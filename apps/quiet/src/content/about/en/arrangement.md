The [contents](/en/contents/) follow the order of the 2012 [Pocket Guide](guide:): first the shiurim in Yiddish, then those in English, and last the [recordings the guide does not list](/en/contents/#uncatalogued).

Every shiur has its own page. At the top is the shiur as the catalog lists it, with the recording
to listen to or download. Below a rule come the [notes](#notes): a summary, the order of the shiur with its
times, the shiur written up, the sources, and the stories, questions and points. Every time on the
page is a link that plays the recording from that point.

The [calendar](/en/calendar/) lists the shiurim by [parsha](/en/calendar/parsha/), by [zman](/en/calendar/zman/) and by [year](/en/years/). The [indexes](/en/#indexes) at the back list the
[sources](/en/sources/), the [stories](/en/stories/), the [people](/en/people/) and the [inyanim](/en/inyanim/), each with the shiurim and the time where it comes
up. There is also a [search](/en/search/).

There are two editions, in [Yiddish](/yi/) and in [English](/en/), with the same shiurim. The edition changes the
language of the site and of the notes, not the language of the shiurim.
