The [contents](/en/contents/) list the shiurim by section: first the shiurim in Yiddish, then those
in English, then [more recordings](/en/contents/#more), and last the shiurim given as a series, in
order.

Every shiur has its own page, with the recording to listen to or download, a short account of
what the shiur is about, and its topics. The account and the topics are [notes](#notes) written
by AI, and the page says so. Links lead to the shiur before it and the shiur after it.

The [calendar](/en/calendar/) lists the shiurim by [parsha](/en/calendar/parsha/), by [zman](/en/calendar/zman/) and by [year](/en/years/). The [indexes](/en/#indexes) at the back list the
[sources](/en/sources/), the [stories](/en/stories/), the [people](/en/people/) and the [inyanim](/en/inyanim/), each with the shiurim and the time where it comes
up. There is also a [search](/en/search/).

There are two editions, in [Yiddish](/yi/) and in [English](/en/), with the same shiurim. The edition changes the
language of the site and of the notes, not the language of the shiurim.
