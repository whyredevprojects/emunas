The site is set out like a sefer. The contents follow the order of the 2012 Pocket Guide: first
the shiurim in Yiddish, then those in English, and last the recordings the guide does not list.

Every shiur has its own page. At the top is the shiur as the catalog lists it, with the recording
to listen to or download. Below a rule come the notes: a summary, the order of the shiur with its
times, the shiur written up, the sources, and the stories, questions and points. Every time on the
page is a link that plays the recording from that point.

The calendar lists the shiurim by parsha, by zman and by year. The indexes at the back list the
sources, the stories, the people and the inyanim, each with the shiurim and the time where it comes
up. There is also a search.

There are two editions, in Yiddish and in English, with the same shiurim. The edition changes the
language of the site and of the notes, not the language of the shiurim.
