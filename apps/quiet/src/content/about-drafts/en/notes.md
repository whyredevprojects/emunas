The notes on each shiur page were written by an AI model from the shiur: the summary, the order
of the shiur, the write-up, the sources, the stories and the other points.

No person has reviewed the notes, and they can contain mistakes. A name, a source or a time can be
wrong, and a point can come out stronger or weaker than the Mashgiach said it.

The notes are not the Mashgiach's words. They report what he says and do not quote him. Where the notes
and the recording differ, the recording is right.

The search looks in the catalog and the notes.

Items the model itself marked as uncertain are left out. A source the model inferred is marked
"apparently"; where only the sefer is known and not the place in it, it says "place not
identified".

Notes are added as they are prepared. A shiur that has no notes yet still has its place in the
catalog and its recording.
