The notes on each shiur page, the short account of what the shiur is about and its topics, were
written by an AI model from the shiur. So were the indexes of [inyanim](/en/inyanim/),
the [glossary](/en/erachim/), [people](/en/people/), [sources](/en/sources/) and [stories](/en/stories/).

No person has reviewed the notes, and they can contain mistakes. A name, a source or a time can be
wrong, and a point can come out stronger or weaker than the Mashgiach said it.

The notes are not the Mashgiach's words. They report what he says and do not quote him. Where the notes
and the recording differ, the recording is right.

The [search](/en/search/) looks in the titles and the notes.

Items the model itself marked as uncertain are left out. In the index of sources, a source the
model inferred is marked "apparently"; where only the sefer is known and not the place in it, it
says "place not identified".

Notes are added as they are prepared. A shiur that has no notes yet still has its page and its
recording.
